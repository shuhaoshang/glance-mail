Glance Visual 2.0 · reference states

Static pages exported from the design prototype, one per screen state. They show structure and look; they are not clickable. Exact values are in docs/design-system.md and src/design-system/tokens/tokens.css. Matching screenshots are in docs/screens/v2/.

Desktop, 1280 wide

  • Needs you (Maya) — The default screen: tabs, the list panel and the detail panel.
  • Needs you · a source open — Selecting an underlined value opens the sentence it came from.
  • Needs you · Glance’s reasoning open — The Why pill, open, with evidence and the Watchlist line.
  • Needs you · Move to menu — Move to lists the three email tabs. The current one is checked and is not a button.
  • Needs you · Original email — The original email with every fact Glance used highlighted.
  • Needs you · Ask — Prepared questions; answers come only from email.
  • Needs you · done and snoozed — Done and snoozed emails leave a row with Undo under the list. The next email is selected.
  • Heads-up — Rows are selectable. The detail shows Glance’s summary, the source and a Watchlist link.
  • Heads-up · after a move, with the toast — A moved email joins the end of the list, marked Moved by you. The toast offers Undo.
  • Other mail — Each row says why Glance put it here. The detail is the original email with highlights.
  • Watchlist — Things the person is waiting on. N new opens the related email.
  • Watchlist · adding an item — Add opens an inline row with a text input.
  • Memory — Instructions and learned patterns. Both can be edited or removed.
  • Account menu — Inboxes, Settings, Appearance and Log out.
  • Settings — Appearance switches the theme. Other controls keep their state for the session.
  • Signed out — What Log out leads to.
  • Needs you (Daniel) — Two inboxes, a quiet hours note and Gmail.
  • Heads-up (Daniel) · Personal label — Only the item from the less common inbox carries an account label.
  • Needs you (Sarah) — Family label and Yahoo Mail.
  • Dark · Needs you — Ink flips to light; panels get a faint edge.
