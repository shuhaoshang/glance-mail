## Detail views

[DetailPanel](?path=/docs/composites-detailpanel--docs) · [DetailSheet](?path=/docs/composites-detailsheet--docs) · [EmailView](?path=/docs/composites-emailview--docs) · [ReasonPanel](?path=/docs/composites-reasonpanel--docs)

The desktop DetailPanel extends the existing detail card with Details and Original email tabs. Details composes the selected item's SceneCard, exact fact sources, and the ReasonPill disclosure. ReasonPanel separates Glance's ranking and judgments from the sender's words, with supporting email excerpts where the data provides them.

DetailSheet presents the same content as a modal on phone. It supports closing with its close button, the scrim, Escape, or a downward handle drag. Focus stays inside while the sheet is open and returns to the row that opened it. The sheet and scrim use the documented motion tokens and become instant with reduced motion.

EmailView renders the supplied subject, metadata, and exact email paragraphs. Its legend identifies the four highlight types, and the Highlighter switch draws or clears the ink. Highlight labels are available on hover and keyboard focus.
