## Visual 2.0 rows and details

[HeadsUpRow](?path=/docs/composites-headsuprow--docs) · [OtherMailRow](?path=/docs/composites-othermailrow--docs) · [MailList](?path=/docs/composites-maillist--docs) · [Rows and details examples](?path=/story/composites-mail-rows-and-details--maya-heads-up)

Heads-up rows open details with a persistent source and an Original email tab. Other mail shows its reason, basis, original paragraphs, and a legend limited to the highlight kinds present. ## Watchlist

[WatchRow](?path=/docs/composites-watchrow--docs) · [WatchlistPanel](?path=/docs/composites-watchlistpanel--docs) · [Watchlist flows](?path=/story/patterns-watchlist--maya)

WatchRow presents the supplied topic name, status, news, and rename/remove controls. News pills reuse tint-button states; icon controls reuse IconButton states. Its rename form uses InlineForm, rejecting blank input and returning focus to Rename. WatchlistPanel composes these rows, the add form, and the empty state. Only one edit form is active at a time. The app owns session changes, Toast Undo, counts, and email navigation.

## Detail views

[AllDone](?path=/docs/composites-alldone--docs) · [SnoozeMenu](?path=/docs/composites-snoozemenu--docs) · [DetailPanel](?path=/docs/composites-detailpanel--docs) · [DetailSheet](?path=/docs/composites-detailsheet--docs) · [EmailView](?path=/docs/composites-emailview--docs) · [ReasonPanel](?path=/docs/composites-reasonpanel--docs)

The desktop DetailPanel extends the existing detail card with Details, Original email, and Ask tabs. Details composes the selected item's SceneCard, exact fact sources, and the ReasonPill disclosure. ReasonPanel separates Glance's ranking and judgments from the sender's words, with supporting email excerpts where the data provides them.

DetailSheet presents the same content as a modal on phone. It supports closing with its close button, the scrim, Escape, or a downward handle drag. Focus stays inside while the sheet is open and returns to the row that opened it. The sheet follows the handle during a drag, then dismisses at the documented distance or velocity threshold or returns over `--duration-fast`. With reduced motion, direct dragging remains available and automatic motion is instant.

EmailView renders the supplied subject, metadata, and exact email paragraphs. Its legend identifies only the highlight types present in that email, and the Highlighter switch draws or clears the ink. Highlight labels are available on hover and keyboard focus.

[ReasonPill](?path=/docs/primitives-reasonpill--docs) · [SourcePanel](?path=/docs/primitives-sourcepanel--docs) · [Scene cards](?path=/docs/scene-cards-overview--docs)

[AskThread](?path=/docs/composites-askthread--docs) offers prepared questions, attributed answers with judgment sources, and a plain not-found answer when the email does not say. The demo note replaces a free-text input.

## New mail

[NewMailRow](?path=/docs/composites-newmailrow--docs) becomes a plain acknowledgement after adding mail.

[Notice](?path=/docs/composites-notice--docs) presents an inbox coverage warning and can offer one primary action.

## Memory

[MemoryRow](?path=/docs/composites-memoryrow--docs) displays instructions and learned patterns with their original metadata. It reuses InlineForm for edits and the shared icon buttons for Edit and Remove. [MemoryPanel](?path=/docs/composites-memorypanel--docs) groups the rows and provides the add and edit forms. The [Memory patterns](?path=/story/patterns-memory--maya) show the panel in the app and the promotion of a learned pattern to an instruction.

## Account menu

[AccountMenu](?path=/docs/composites-accountmenu--docs) presents account identity and Appearance. The avatar opens a menu with System, Light, and Dark choices. The phone layout places the choices below the label, and keyboard focus returns to the avatar on dismissal.

## Settings and sign-in

[SettingsPanel](?path=/docs/composites-settingspanel--docs) groups account information, session preferences, and Appearance. It uses [TimeSelect](?path=/docs/primitives-timeselect--docs) for the three native time controls. [SignedOutPanel](?path=/docs/composites-signedoutpanel--docs) contains the two demo sign-in choices and the inbox privacy note.
