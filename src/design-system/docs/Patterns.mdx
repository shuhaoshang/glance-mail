# Patterns

[Pattern specifications](https://github.com/shuhaoshang/glance-mail/blob/main/docs/design-system.md#6-patterns)

## Brief

[Maya](?path=/story/patterns-brief--maya) · [Daniel](?path=/story/patterns-brief--daniel) · [Sarah](?path=/story/patterns-brief--sarah)

The brief uses persona data at desktop, tablet, and phone widths. Desktop keeps the selected item's DetailPanel alongside the list. On phone, selecting a row opens its DetailSheet; closing the sheet returns focus to that row and removes the selected appearance. Set aside opens grouped mail in the desktop detail area or the same phone sheet.

## Reading and verifying an item

[DetailPanel](?path=/docs/composites-detailpanel--docs) · [DetailSheet](?path=/docs/composites-detailsheet--docs) · [EmailView](?path=/docs/composites-emailview--docs) · [ReasonPanel](?path=/docs/composites-reasonpanel--docs)

Details and Original email stay inside the same detail card or sheet. Selecting an underlined SceneCard value opens the corresponding SourcePanel. Its Open email link switches to Original email, where the Highlighter control reveals the facts Glance used. Glance's reasoning remains a separate disclosure so its judgments are not presented as the sender's words.

Open in Gmail, Outlook, or Yahoo Mail opens the corresponding web inbox in a new tab. Ask and lifecycle actions belong to their later tasks and are not shown as inactive controls.

## Demo controls and levels

[Demo bar and Glance](?path=/story/patterns-demo--glance) · [Plain AI text](?path=/story/patterns-demo--plain) · [Formatted text](?path=/story/patterns-demo--formatted)

The demo bar is separate from the product brief. Switching personas preserves the level, and switching levels preserves the persona. The app writes persona changes to the URL; browser history restores the persona without resetting the current level. Level 3 is the default when the app loads.

The narrow layout uses a Demo disclosure below the existing desktop breakpoint. The same controls appear under the bar and stay open until the toggle or Escape closes them. The app’s [theme switch](?path=/story/patterns-demo--theme-selection) uses System, Light, and Dark. System follows the browser’s color scheme live. Light and Dark override it, and the app remembers the choice when browser storage is available. Use the Storybook theme toolbar to change the palette of this control example.

## Completing and correcting the brief

[Item lifecycle](?path=/story/patterns-item-lifecycle--maya) includes Done, Snooze, Undo, simulated replies, checklist completion, and the empty brief. The Maya, Daniel, and Sarah flow stories verify their documented counts. A snoozed item remains an unfinished action; a moved item leaves the action total and increases Set aside.

## Set aside and new mail

[Set aside flows](?path=/story/patterns-set-aside--maya) verify keeping mail, undoing, restoring Needs you items, and Maya’s explicit new-mail update. New items append without reordering the existing list. Kept whole emails appear in Good to know with “Moved by you”.
