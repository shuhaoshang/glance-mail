# Patterns

[Pattern specifications](https://github.com/shuhaoshang/glance-mail/blob/main/docs/design-system.md#6-patterns)

## Brief

[Maya](?path=/story/patterns-brief--maya) · [Daniel](?path=/story/patterns-brief--daniel) · [Sarah](?path=/story/patterns-brief--sarah)

The brief uses persona data at desktop, tablet, and phone widths. Desktop keeps the selected item's DetailPanel alongside the list. On phone, selecting a row opens its DetailSheet; closing the sheet returns focus to that row and removes the selected appearance. Set aside remains a static card until task 12.

## Reading and verifying an item

[DetailPanel](?path=/docs/composites-detailpanel--docs) · [DetailSheet](?path=/docs/composites-detailsheet--docs) · [EmailView](?path=/docs/composites-emailview--docs) · [ReasonPanel](?path=/docs/composites-reasonpanel--docs)

Details and Original email stay inside the same detail card or sheet. Selecting an underlined SceneCard value opens the corresponding SourcePanel. Its Open email link switches to Original email, where the Highlighter control reveals the facts Glance used. Glance's reasoning remains a separate disclosure so its judgments are not presented as the sender's words.

Open in Gmail, Outlook, or Yahoo Mail opens the corresponding web inbox in a new tab. Ask and lifecycle actions belong to their later tasks and are not shown as inactive controls.

## Demo controls and levels

[Demo bar and Glance](?path=/story/patterns-demo--glance) · [Plain AI text](?path=/story/patterns-demo--plain) · [Formatted text](?path=/story/patterns-demo--formatted)

The demo bar is separate from the product brief. Switching personas preserves the level, and switching levels preserves the persona. The app writes persona changes to the URL; browser history restores the persona without resetting the current level. Level 3 is the default when the app loads.

The narrow layout uses a Demo disclosure below the existing desktop breakpoint. The same controls appear under the bar and stay open until the toggle or Escape closes them. Later tasks add reply simulation and theme controls through the bar's extra-controls slot.
